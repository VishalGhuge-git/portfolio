## What it does

The core capabilities I defined for the MVP:

- **Auto-furnish** an empty room from a photo
- **Swap furniture and styles** in an existing room
- **Restyle any space** from an uploaded image

## How I ran it

- **Owned the 0-to-1 product strategy** for the platform.
- **Onboarded and directed a UI/UX agency** in Figma.
- **Ran user research and discovery** to validate the AI interaction patterns and de-risk decisions before MVP handoff.

## Outcome

An MVP in 5 months, with the AI interaction patterns validated through research before handoff. Product site: ideate.foyr.com.
