## Context

Foyr Neo is Foyr's existing interior design software. Ideate was a bet that AI could take over the slowest, most subjective part of the work: **exploring ideas** before any detailed design begins.

## Users and their jobs

| User | Job to be done |
| --- | --- |
| **Designers** (existing Foyr customers) | Explore and show design directions quickly, without building each one by hand |
| **Homeowners** | See what their own space could look like, in an old room or a completely new one |

## The problem

Ideating is where design work stalls. A designer has to build every option before a client can react to it, and a homeowner can't picture a result from a floor plan or a description. The goal was to make **designing and ideating easier**, whether the space is an existing room or a blank one.

## The MVP: what it does

I defined the core capabilities around the two situations users start from:

- **Empty room:** auto-furnish it
- **Existing room:** redesign it, or **replace, remove and add** individual pieces of furniture

## The decision that shaped the business: pricing

An AI feature has a real cost per use, so pricing could not be an afterthought. I worked through **different plans and how they would be structured** as part of the MVP, because the plan design decides who can use the product and whether it can sustain itself.

## Working with an external agency

I directed an external UI/UX agency in Figma. The most useful lesson was where to spend attention: I gave **direction and explained the vision**, and focused on the **high-level UI direction, not small friction points**. Chasing details early would have slowed the team and hidden the big decisions.

## Outcome

- **MVP in 5 months**, ready for handoff, with the AI interaction patterns validated through prototype testing
- A live product at [ideate.foyr.com](https://ideate.foyr.com)
