## The problem

Fractal is a thousands-person organisation with hundreds active bench members, who have to be matched to open demand. That matching had to get **faster**, and internal hiring needed **higher conversion**. That was the brief for the platform: shorter staffing turnaround for the talent team, and more hires closed.

## What I built

A single platform that matches bench employees to open demand, and supports hiring and bench assessment. I defined the product strategy and roadmap, and led a 7-member cross-functional team to ship it.

## Six AI agents, and how I prioritised them

I defined the use cases for the agents and set the build order by **impact on the hiring funnel**, then integrated each into the platform.

- **Interview Bot** for lateral hiring
- **Practice Bot** for fresher hiring
- **JD Bot** for creating job descriptions and matching candidates
- **Case Study Bot** and **Debrief Bot** for bench assessment
- **Candidate Agent**
