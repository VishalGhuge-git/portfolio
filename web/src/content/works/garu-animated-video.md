**TL;DR** An AI-assisted 2D animated video series concept, built to grow a personal brand as a builder and product manager. I designed the character, the tone and a production workflow short enough to repeat, and started with a single pilot video before committing to a series.

## Context and user problem

I wanted a distinctive, creative way to share what I build and think about as a product manager, in an animated, old-school style with its own character.

## Goal

Make one video that clearly shows the style, then decide whether to commit to a series. The workflow had to be sustainable: one video in one hour at most.

## Decision and rationale

I chose to try **one video first** before committing to a series. Two creative decisions define the channel:

- **Two personas:** a builder and a product manager, to match who I am.
- **Garu:** a small, cute pet inspired by Garuda from Indian mythology. He sounds wise, not hyper, and his voice matches his small, young look. The pilot uses two voices: my character and Garu.

The overall tone is calm, deep and thoughtful, with an old-school aesthetic, aiming for quality that matches or beats the 3D low-poly clay reels that inspired it.

## What I shipped

An AI-assisted animated video built around Garu and the two-voice format, plus a production workflow designed to produce one video in about an hour.

## What I would do differently

I would define what a good first result looks like in advance, for example views, saves and follows, so the decision to continue to a series is based on a signal and not a feeling.
