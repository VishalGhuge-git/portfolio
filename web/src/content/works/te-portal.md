## Context

This is the second half of one pipeline. The client's designers build in a 3D design tool (the B2B design tool, covered separately). **That 3D data flows into a customer-facing web portal** where buyers make their selections.

## The problem

Premium home buyers face **a huge number of combinations**: room sizes, design language, flooring, textures, furniture, electrical and more. The real problem was not showing options, it was **educating the buyer on everything that is possible** before asking them to choose.

## The strategy: educate, then configure

I framed the product around one idea: **show buyers what is possible, in their own home, before asking them to choose.** The portal lets a buyer:

- Explore the home in **3D**
- Choose **room sizes**
- Pick a **design language**
- Select **flooring and textures**
- Choose **furniture**
- Configure **electrical** layouts

## Outcome

Per the sales team, the portal contributed to:

- **Faster conversion**
- **Higher sales**
- **Stronger buyer confidence** across premium residential and villa developments
